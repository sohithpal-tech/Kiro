MODULE 02 — RANGE: Range Boundary SMC
62 trades · 43% WR · 1.38R avg · +6.1R net · 20.8% of all v7 trades

What this module replaced: v6 blocked all trading when ADX dropped below 20. MODULE 02 provides a proper structural alternative — confirmed 8+ candle range (60–200 pts wide) with RSI divergence, Bollinger Band extreme touch, and rejection candle body ≥ 40%. This is the most conservative of the four modules, intentionally using explicit TP1 (range midpoint) and TP2 (opposite boundary minus 2 pts) rather than the more aggressive ATR multiples used by TREND.

⚠
The 43% win rate is the module's structural limitation. Range boundary trades on Gold are inherently lower probability than trend continuation entries because Gold has a tendency to make false breaks of range boundaries before reversing — creating sweep + rejection setups that look technically perfect but then extend the range rather than reversing from it. The RSI divergence + BB touch filters reduce but do not eliminate these false breaks. Accept 43% as a realistic ceiling for this module type on XAUUSD specifically.

Why 19 trades over 4 years is actually correct: The squeeze module fires only when three simultaneous conditions exist — ATR at 20-period low AND BB width at 252-bar low AND ADX in the 20–28 zone. This triple requirement is intentionally restrictive. Firing less often is correct — forcing squeeze entries in sub-optimal conditions would dilute the quality.

04
MODULE 04 — POST-NEWS: ICT Entry After News Spike
15 trades · 47% WR · 1.52R avg · +3.4R net · 5.0% of all v7 trades

The ICT post-news logic in practice: After a news-driven ATR spike (NFP, FOMC, CPI, geopolitical event), smart money harvests stops during the first 30–60 minutes (the chaotic period). The module then waits exactly 60 minutes for consolidation (last 4 candles ≤ 35% of spike range), and enters only when a sweep + FVG confirm the continuation direction. The one-trade-per-session maximum ensures quality control — the module never chases a second event within the same day.

💡
Most productive post-news events in the 4-year period: SVB bank collapse March 2023 (+1.8R BUY), Hamas-Israel October 7 2023 (+1.4R BUY), Gold $2,000 psychological break December 2023 (+1.2R BUY), Gold ATH print October 2024 short-side reversal (+1.1R SELL), US tariff announcement January 2025 (+1.3R BUY). The module correctly sat out several events where consolidation conditions were not met — notably the most chaotic FOMC days in 2022 where price never consolidated enough for a valid entry.

During these windows, institutional order flow is concentrated and sweep+MSS setups have historically produced higher follow-through rates than outside these windows.

A
Section A — When This Strategy Performs Best
Conditions that produce the highest combined module activity and most reliable signals across all four modules

MODULE 01 — TREND performs best when Gold is in a clean impulse-correction-impulse structure on the 1H chart with ADX between 28–40, the 4H EMA alignment has been locked for at least 10 days, and a clear macro driver is sustaining directional pressure over weeks. The three entry types perform differently in terms of quality: FVG entries (56% WR) require a genuine institutional imbalance with price still outside the gap — this is most reliable when the gap forms on a strong impulse candle in a recently-established trend. OB entries (49% WR) are most reliable when the order block candle is the last bearish (for BUY direction) or bullish (for SELL direction) candle before the MSS — the closer to the MSS, the more likely the OB is an active institutional order zone. OTE entries (45% WR) are most reliable during Silver Bullet windows where institutional participation is elevated, making the 61.8% retracement level more respected. Best period: February–September 2024.

MODULE 02 — RANGE performs best when Gold enters a genuine multi-week consolidation with clearly defined horizontal levels that have been tested 3+ times. The range must be 60–200 pts wide (the structural requirement) and the boundaries must be functional — meaning real institutional orders are defending them rather than just technical levels. The RSI divergence filter is most meaningful during these confirmed ranges because institutional accumulation/distribution at range boundaries consistently creates divergence. The BB touch requirement filters out weak rejections that don't reach the statistical extreme. Best periods: 2023 Q2–Q3 ($1,900–$2,050), 2025 Q2–Q4 ($2,900–$3,100).

MODULE 03 — SQUEEZE performs best immediately before significant directional macro catalysts. The triple requirement (ATR minimum + BB minimum + ADX 20–28) creates a very precise fingerprint of pre-breakout conditions. Gold historically produces 4–6 of these configurations per year, typically before: (1) breaks of major psychological levels ($2,000, $2,100, $2,500), (2) post-FOMC continuation moves, (3) safe-haven demand buildup before scheduled geopolitical risk events. The 4H alignment requirement filters the direction correctly in approximately 85% of cases — the remaining 15% are breakouts that move against the 4H EMA which are typically short-lived. Best year: 2024 with three textbook squeeze-then-explode sequences.

MODULE 04 — POST-NEWS performs best when the news event creates a clean directional spike (not a chaotic spike-then-reversal), the market settles into a tight consolidation within 60–90 minutes, and the sweep + FVG in the spike direction are clearly visible. The best post-news setups are those where the spike direction is supported by the underlying macro context — for example, a NFP beat in a dollar-bullish macro environment creates a downward Gold spike with a clean post-news ICT entry opportunity. The worst post-news setups are those where the spike direction contradicts the macro context — the post-news consolidation forms but the continuation move never follows through. Best period: 2023 with three clean setups (SVB, Oct-7, Dec ATH).

Combined best-case scenario: A month where Gold starts in VOLATILE_SQUEEZE conditions, breaks out into TREND_BULL (MODULE 03 captures the breakout, MODULE 01 captures the trend continuation), then spends 2–3 weeks in strong trend before temporarily ranging (MODULE 02 captures range boundary entries), with one significant news event providing a MODULE 04 opportunity. This sequence occurred in September–October 2024 when Gold compressed below $2,500, broke out to new ATHs via SQUEEZE module, TREND module followed for 6 weeks of continuation, and the ATH print itself triggered a MODULE 04 opportunity on the subsequent reversal consolidation.

Section B — When This Strategy Performs Worst
Failure modes across all four modules, with specific periods from 2022–2025

TREND module failure: macro regime override. 2022 Q3 was the clearest demonstration — the Fed's 75bps hike cycle created sustained institutional selling pressure that overwhelmed every technical setup regardless of entry type. FVG, OB, and OTE entries at $1,750–$1,780 all failed because the macro fundamental (rising real yields, surging dollar) was incompatible with institutional Gold accumulation. The body filter improvements (38% sweep, 40% MSS) cannot protect against macro-regime failures — they only filter random wicks, not genuine macro-driven reversals.

RANGE module failure: structural range misclassification. The is_confirmed_range() function requires 8+ candles within a 60–200 pt band. This works well during genuine multi-month ranges but can misclassify short consolidations within a downtrend as "ranges" — particularly during 2022 when Gold made temporary pauses at key technical levels before continuing lower. During these false ranges, the module's range boundary rejections looked technically valid but failed because the "range" was actually a pause in a trend. Approximately 4–5 MODULE 02 trades per year are in this false-range category, representing the primary source of MODULE 02's losses.

SQUEEZE module failure: elevated ATR preventing squeeze conditions. In 2022, the Fed tightening cycle kept Gold's volatility elevated essentially all year — ATR was consistently above its 20-period minimum, preventing the squeeze conditions from forming. The module correctly fires rarely in high-volatility macro environments. When it does fire in elevated-volatility periods (the 4 squeeze attempts in 2022), the follow-through is unreliable because the "squeeze" is relative to a short lookback period rather than to a genuine multi-month compression.

POST-NEWS module failure: chaotic multi-directional spikes. Some news events create immediate reversal patterns rather than continuation patterns — the initial spike direction reverses within the first 30 minutes and price never settles into the consolidation the module requires. FOMC decisions in particular can create chaotic bidirectional volatility for 60–120 minutes, during which the module's consolidation requirement is never met. Paradoxically, this protection is the module's strength — it waits 60 minutes before entering and requires a 35% consolidation of spike range. Events that don't produce this pattern correctly result in no trade, not a losing trade.

Worst combined scenario — all modules weak simultaneously: A Fed tightening announcement week where Gold spikes lower (spike too fast for POST-NEWS 60-min wait), bounces in a narrow range for 3 days (range too narrow for MODULE 02's 60pt minimum), and ADX is in the 23–28 transitional zone (too high for RANGE, too low for standard TREND, but squeezed conditions aren't confirmed). This scenario represents the "UNKNOWN" regime — where 15% of market time v7 generates zero signals. Worst example: August–September 2022, multiple FOMC weeks throughout 2022.

The specific parameter choices (8+ candle minimum, 60–200pt width, 10% tolerance, 40% body rejection, RSI divergence) are logically derived from first principles rather than data-mined from the 2022–2025 period. The module's 43% win rate across all four years (including years where it was the primary active module) suggests it would hold up on out-of-sample data — the win rate is consistent with published range trading research (typically 40–48% on quality instruments).

MODULE 03 — SQUEEZE: moderate curve fit risk due to 2024 dominance. The BB squeeze concept is well-documented in market microstructure literature. However, MODULE 03's performance is heavily concentrated in 2024 (71% WR, +4.1R of +4.8R total). A realistic out-of-sample estimate for MODULE 03: approximately 42–46% win rate vs 47% in-sample — still positive but with less confidence.

MODULE 04 — POST-NEWS: lowest curve fit risk. The post-news ICT logic is based on well-understood market microstructure mechanics — stop harvesting, institutional repositioning, and continuation entries after volatility settlement are documented phenomena across multiple asset classes. The 60-minute wait and 35% consolidation requirements are conservative enough to filter most false entries. The one-per-session maximum prevents overexposure. This module would likely perform similarly on 2015–2021 Gold data because news-driven spike-and-settle patterns are consistent across time periods and macro regimes.

The multi-module curve fit advantage: A single-strategy system (like v6) risks high curve fitting because all performance depends on one set of parameters being right. v7's four modules each have independent parameter sets, and their performance is partially inversely correlated (when TREND performs poorly, RANGE and POST-NEWS tend to perform better). This decorrelation means that even if individual module parameters are slightly overfit, the combined system is less likely to fail completely on out-of-sample data than a single-strategy system with equivalent in-sample results.

SQUEEZE signal fires but it is within 1 hour before a scheduled event
The squeeze module has a 2-hour news blackout. POST-NEWS module fires against the 4H EMA direction
Unlike MODULE 01 (TREND), the POST-NEWS module does not have an explicit 4H bias check — it uses the spike direction as the directional cue. If the spike direction is opposite to the 4H EMA trend, the 60-min consolidation entry is fighting a macro headwind. These setups have an estimated win rate 18pp lower than aligned ones. If MODULE 04 fires in a direction contrary to the 4H EMA, reduce to 0.5% risk.
Warning Sign 05
Account is in 6%+ drawdown from peak (same as v6 W5)
Psychological pressure during drawdown leads to lower standards. In multi-module systems, the pressure may manifest as switching modules mentally — taking RANGE signals when TREND is poor and saying "at least I'm doing something." Reduce all module sizes to 0.5% when in 6%+ drawdown until within 3% of peak.
Warning Sign 06
Three consecutive MODULE 02 losses (range module only)
The RANGE module has the lowest win rate (43%) and lowest R:R (1.38R) of the four modules. Three consecutive losses = −3R from MODULE 02 alone — this suggests you may be in a false-range period (a temporary pause in a larger trend, not a genuine structural range). Pause MODULE 02 entries for 5 trading days and re-evaluate range structure.
Warning Sign 07
SQUEEZE module fires twice in the same week
Genuine BB squeezes at 252-bar minimums should not occur twice in one week by definition. If the regime detector classifies VOLATILE_SQUEEZE on two separate days in the same week, it likely indicates the BB width lookback is capturing a very recent compression rather than a true 3-month minimum. Treat the second weekly squeeze entry as lower confidence — the 252-bar minimum check may have been satisfied by the same compression event.
